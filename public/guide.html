Rovefix User Guide

Welcome to Rovefix! This guide will help you navigate the platform whether you are looking for a repair or offering your professional skills.

🏠 For Homeowners

1

Post a Request: Select a category (Electrician, Plumber, etc.) and describe your issue. You can mark it as 🚨 Emergency for urgent repairs.

2

Review Bids: Nearby technicians will see your post and send their offers. You can see their Star Ratings and experience before choosing.

3

Hire & Verify: Once you accept a bid, you'll get a 4-digit PIN. Give this to the technician only when they arrive at your home.

4

Confirm & Review: After the work is done, the technician will upload a photo. Check it, confirm the job as done, and leave a review to help others!

🔧 For Technicians

1

Verification: To start receiving jobs, upload your Aadhaar (ID) and Professional License in your profile. Our team will review it within 24 hours.

2

Stay Online: Use the toggle on your home screen to stay Online. You'll only see jobs within a 20km radius of your current location.

3

Place Bids: Browse the "Job Feed" and send offers for tasks you can complete. You can also receive Direct Invitations from homeowners.

4

Start Work: When you arrive at the customer's location, ask for their Verification PIN and enter it in the app to officially start the work.

🛡️ Safety Shield Features

  • Arrival PIN: Ensures the correct technician is physically present before the work starts.
  • Proximity Matching: We only connect you with people within 20km for faster service.
  • Two-Way Ratings: Homeowners rate technicians, and technicians rate homeowners to maintain mutual respect.
  • Report System: Any unprofessional behavior can be reported directly through the job status screen.
